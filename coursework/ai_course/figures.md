## Methods represented

The original figures cover regional EDA, Africa/Europe word clouds, TF-IDF/cosine similarity, a joint 10-topic LDA comparison, K-means clustering, LSTM continent classification, VADER sentiment trends/distributions, and positive/negative sentiment word clouds.

The professional reconstruction of these methods is organized in [`../../notebooks/02_africa_europe_ml.ipynb`](../../notebooks/02_africa_europe_ml.ipynb) and [`../../src/comparative_analysis.py`](../../src/comparative_analysis.py).
